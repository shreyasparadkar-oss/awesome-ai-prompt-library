# Study Plan Generator

## Prompt

Act as an expert study planner.

Create a realistic study plan for me based on:
- Subject: [subject]
- Topics: [topics]
- Available time: [hours per day]
- Exam date: [date]
- Current level: [beginner/intermediate/advanced]

Break the plan into daily tasks and include revision, practice, and short breaks.

Keep the plan realistic and easy to follow.
