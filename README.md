## 🚀 How to Use

1. Choose a category.
2. Open the prompt you need.
3. Copy the prompt.
4. Paste it into your preferred AI tool.
5. Replace the `[PLACEHOLDERS]` with your information.
6. Get your result and refine it if needed.
