# Goal Planner

## Prompt

Act as a practical goal-setting coach.

Help me create a plan for:

Goal:
[GOAL]

Current situation:
[CURRENT SITUATION]

Available time:
[TIME]

Deadline:
[DATE]

Break the goal into:

* Major milestones
* Weekly objectives
* Daily actions
* Progress checkpoints

Make the plan specific, realistic, and easy to track.
